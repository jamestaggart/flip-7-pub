# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project aims to be welcoming, respectful, and practical.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.

Unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or bad-faith engagement.

## Enforcement

Maintainer may edit, remove, or reject content that violates this code of conduct.
Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from further participation.

## Reporting

Open an issue with context, or contact the maintainer through repository discussions if enabled.
